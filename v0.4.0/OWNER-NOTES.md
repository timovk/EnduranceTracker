# EnduranceTracker 0.4.0 — notes for you

0.4.0 is "Your career, year by year": the Chronicle and Endurance Wrapped, a page for every recurring event,
Race Expeditions, Career Statistics with personal records, and Career Milestones. A few of your decisions met in
places where following one would have broken another, and a few things behave in a way you might not guess.
Here they are, in plain words.

1. **"First race started" has no XP of its own.** Your first stint already earns Green Flag and the
   first-session milestone for that exact moment, and you asked for nothing to pay twice. It is shown on the
   Milestones page with its date.

2. **"First edition experienced" of an event has no XP either**, so that a race put into an event of its own
   cannot earn a step for a few clicks. Every later event step pays a little.

3. **5, 10 and 25 editions of one event** pay through that event's own steps, once per event; the career
   milestone shows the moment without paying it again. **2,500 hours** is paid by The Archive achievement.

4. **Moving a race to a different event never pays a step twice.** If a race was linked to the wrong event and
   you move it, the steps the wrong event had already reached with it are not paid again in the right one.

5. **"Races experienced"** means you watched at least a tenth of the race (or an hour of a long one) and at
   least ten minutes in all.

6. **Switching Expedition Mode off keeps the checkpoint XP you earned.** Checkpoint XP comes back off only if
   you delete the viewing (or the race) behind it.

7. **A finished year is settled about three days into January**, so a race you watch across New Year counts
   fully in both years. Until then its chapter says "finalising".

Two more things worth knowing:

- **On a very long history, the Chronicle can take up to about a second to open the first time after a
  change.** With ten years and 60,000 stints, the Chronicle, or a finished year's chapter, takes between half a
  second and a second the first time after the app starts or after you change something (the aim was half a
  second), and well under a quarter of a second after that.
  Your own career is nowhere near that size, and opens it in a fraction of a second either way.

- **Editing a race just before deleting it can let its edition pay event steps once more.** The app remembers
  a deleted race by its year, its length in whole hours and its circuit or name, so that the same edition added
  to another event cannot pay the event's steps again. If you change its date, or its length by enough to change
  the whole hours, and then delete it, the re-added edition can pay those steps a second time once its viewing is
  logged again. Closing that would mean keeping every race's earlier values, which 0.4.0 does not do.
